Hi! I’m Chaemin Lim, a Ph.D. candidate at Yonsei University / HPCP Lab, advised by Prof. Youngsok Kim.

My research broadly spans systems software, with a primary focus on database systems and hardware-aware acceleration. My work ranges from GPU-, PIM-, and DPU-accelerated data systems to systems software for secure and resource-constrained edge computing.

At Mangoboost, I focused on accelerating prefill–decode (PD) disaggregated LLM serving on AMD GPU clusters, including RDMA-based KV-cache transfer, multi-node serving, and ROCm inference paths, alongside upstream contributions to vLLM.
