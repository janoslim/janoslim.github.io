About

Hi! I’m Chaemin Lim, a Ph.D. candidate at Yonsei University / HPCP Lab, advised by Prof. Youngsok Kim.

My research broadly spans systems software, with a primary focus on database systems. I design hardware-aware execution and data-movement mechanisms for heterogeneous platforms, ranging from GPU-, PIM-, and DPU-accelerated servers to resource-constrained and trusted edge devices.

My goal is to translate emerging hardware capabilities into practical, efficient, and secure end-to-end systems.

Research Interests

  • Systems software, with an emphasis on database systems
  • GPU-accelerated analytical and out-of-memory query execution
  • Near-data processing with PIM and DPU hardware
  • Join processing, skew handling, collective communication, and data movement
  • Secure and resource-constrained edge computing, trusted execution environments, and on-device inference
